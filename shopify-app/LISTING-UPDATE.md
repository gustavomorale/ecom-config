## Testing instructions (max 2800) (2258 characters)

```
NO LOGIN OR CREDENTIALS
There is no separate account or password. The app opens inside the Shopify admin and uses Shopify's own authentication. Installing it gives full access to every feature.

WHERE PRODUCTS COME FROM
The app sells no products, holds no inventory and never creates, imports or edits products. It only reads the store's existing products (read_products). The merchant picks which of their own products the quiz recommends with Shopify's product picker. Shopify's test data (snowboards) works well.

1. INSTALL: install the app and choose the Free plan on Shopify's plan page (development stores are not charged). The welcome page opens. Click Start setup.

2. YOUR PRODUCTS: choose Product finder (one product per shopper) or Bundle quiz (a main product plus extras). Under "What do you sell?" pick the closest match (it only suggests question wording). Click Choose products and pick 3 to 6 of your products in Shopify's picker. Save and continue.

3. QUESTIONS: rename a question or answer. Under each answer, click the product chips to choose which products it recommends (selected chips turn blue). The picture button left of an answer adds an emoji or a product photo. The preview shows the quiz as shoppers see it. Save and continue.

4. GO LIVE: click Open theme editor, then Add section, Apps, CraftFrame Bundle Quiz, and Save the theme. Back in the app, click Finish setup. Home shows the quiz as Live.

5. STOREFRONT: open the page with the block and answer the questions. The result shows the best-fitting product with its real name, photo and price. Add to cart opens the cart or checkout with exactly that product.

6. AFTER SETUP: Home lists each quiz with this month's completions and the plan (changed on Shopify's plan page). Look and Copy & cart change the colours and every word shoppers read.

UNINSTALL: the block disappears from the storefront. Session data is deleted; shop data is deleted on Shopify's shop/redact request.

ACCESS: read_products, read_themes. The app writes app metafields on the shop only and stores no customer data. Privacy policy: https://bundle-configurator.netlify.app/privacy
Support: contact@craftframe.agency
Screencast (3 min, English narration and subtitles): https://youtu.be/VPd381BuGe4
```
