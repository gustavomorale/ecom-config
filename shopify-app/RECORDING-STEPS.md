# Recording steps (follow this while recording)

Store: **bundle-quiz-review-oct** · App 1.0.8 · Chrome window 1760 x 990 · About 4 minutes in the
final cut. Narration is in `SCREENCAST.md` (record the voice later or read it live).

Rules while recording: move the mouse slowly, pause about 1 second before every click, never
rush. Where it says **STOP**, stop recording (Cmd + Ctrl + Esc, or the stop button in the menu bar),
do the work off camera, then start a new recording.

---

## Before you start (off camera)

1. Storefront unlocked once in this Chrome: open https://bundle-quiz-review-oct.myshopify.com
   and enter the password.
2. Copy this install link (you will paste it in step 6):
   `https://admin.shopify.com/store/bundle-quiz-review-oct/oauth/install?client_id=a1b72ae7a95a6312f1f0587db9042df4`
3. Open https://admin.shopify.com/store/bundle-quiz-review-oct/products
4. Close every other tab in this window. Notifications off (Focus mode).

---

## Recording 1: the store's products, install, plan, welcome (about 1 minute)

**Start recording:** Cmd + Shift + 5, Record Selected Portion, drag over the Chrome window, Record.

5. **Products page.** Hold still 3 seconds. Move the pointer slowly down the list of snowboards
   and back up. *(This shows where every product in the quiz comes from.)*
6. Click the address bar, paste the install link, press **Enter**.
7. **Install screen.** Hover over the permissions ("View products", "View themes") for 2 seconds.
   Click **Install**.
8. **Plan page.** Pause 2 seconds on the four plans. Choose **Free**, then approve.
9. **Welcome page.** Pause. Scroll down slowly past the example, the three steps, "What you get"
   and the plans. Scroll back to the top. Click **Start setup**.
10. Wait until **Your products** has loaded. **STOP.**

---

## Recording 2: Your products (about 1 minute)

**Start recording.**

11. Hover over **Product finder**, then over **Bundle quiz**. Click **Product finder**.
12. **What do you sell?** Open the list, choose **Sports & outdoors**.
13. Click **Choose products**. Shopify's product picker opens: pause 2 seconds on the list.
14. Tick these five, slowly:
    - The Collection Snowboard: Liquid
    - The Collection Snowboard: Hydrogen
    - The Collection Snowboard: Oxygen
    - The Complete Snowboard
    - The Multi-managed Snowboard

16. Click **Save and continue** (top right). Wait for **Check the questions** to load. **STOP.**

| Answer | Emoji | Recommends |
