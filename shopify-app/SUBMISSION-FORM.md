## App store listing content

**App introduction (100):**
Ask shoppers a few questions, recommend the right bundle, open checkout pre-filled.

**App details (500):**
Turn "which one should I buy?" into a five-minute setup and a one-click cart. Shoppers
answer four to six questions in a block on any page; your rules pick the base bundle and
the add-ons that fit; the result shows what is in the set and why, then opens checkout
with everything loaded. Pick a category to start from a working template (skincare, home
security, subscription boxes and more), match your store's colours in one click, link
your products, done. Email support within one working day.

**Features (each 80; the form usually takes 3 to 6):**
1. Category templates: a working questionnaire the moment you pick what you sell
2. Match my store: reads your theme's colours, type and corners in one click
3. Rules in plain rows: no code, quantities can be calculated from answers
4. Options as emoji rows or picture cards from your product photos
5. Result screen with reasons, price preview and a pre-filled checkout link
6. No theme edits, no customer data stored, keyboard and screen reader friendly
