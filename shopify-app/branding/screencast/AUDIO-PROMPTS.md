# Audio generation prompts (ElevenLabs Music and Sound Effects)

## Voice
Paste `voiceover-script.txt` as one generation. Model Eleven Multilingual v2 or v3, calm
mid-paced voice, Stability 50, Similarity 75, Style 0, Speaker boost on. If a break over
3 s is rejected, use two consecutive 3 s breaks.

## Music: `bed.mp3`, 3:25

Instrumental background track for a 3 minute 25 second software product demo with
voiceover. Calm, modern, optimistic. Soft electronic pop with warm piano, muted synth pads,
light plucked guitar and a gentle four-on-the-floor kick. Tempo 96 BPM. No vocals, no lead
melody that competes with speech; keep the mids clear for a voice.
Structure, in seconds: 0 to 30 intro, sparse piano and pad, no drums. 30 to 150 main bed,
drums enter softly at 30, steady low-key groove, one small lift at 100 with a subtle string
layer, then settle. 150 to 185 build, hi-hats open, bass more present, pads swell. 185 to
195 peak, fullest and brightest, chords resolve upward. 195 to 205 settle to piano and pad,
clean ending on one held chord fading by 205.
Mood: clean, trustworthy, effortless, premium. No ukulele, no whistling, no claps.
