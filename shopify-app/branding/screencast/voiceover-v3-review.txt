## scene-01 (Install app screen, before clicking)
CraftFrame Bundle Quiz adds a short questionnaire to a Shopify store. <break time="0.4s" /> Shoppers answer a few questions, the merchant's rules pick a bundle and add-ons from the merchant's own products, and the result opens a filled cart. <break time="0.6s" /> The app sells no products and supplies no inventory. <break time="0.5s" /> I'll set it up from scratch, on a new development store that sells snowboards.

## scene-02 (View store data, Install, plan page, approve, welcome page)
I install the app. <break time="0.4s" /> It asks to view store data: products, so they can be linked, and the online store theme, so the questionnaire can match its look. <break time="0.4s" /> It never edits products, the theme, or orders. <break time="1.0s" /> Shopify then shows the app's plan page. <break time="0.3s" /> There's one plan, Standard, with a fourteen-day free trial. On a development store it's free. <break time="0.6s" /> I approve it, and land on the app's welcome page.

## scene-03 (Start setup, category grid, Sports & outdoors)
Setup has five steps. <break time="0.4s" /> Step one: what are you selling? <break time="0.4s" /> Each category starts a complete, working questionnaire that I then make my own. <break time="0.5s" /> This store sells snowboards, so I'll start from Sports and outdoors.

## scene-04 (Look, Match my store, mobile preview, Save and continue)
Step two makes the questionnaire look like the store. <break time="0.4s" /> Match my store reads the published theme's colours, font and corners, <break time="0.3s" /> and tells me how good the match is. <break time="0.6s" /> I could pick my own brand colour instead. <break time="0.4s" /> The preview updates, on desktop and on mobile. <break time="0.5s" /> Save and continue.

Step three: the questions. <break time="0.4s" /> The template asks about hiking, so I'll reword the first question for snowboarding: how do you ride? <break time="0.5s" /> Learning, all-mountain, or backcountry. <break time="0.8s" /> I'll show these answers as picture cards, <break time="0.3s" /> and for each one choose a picture from my store. <break time="0.3s" /> Shopify's product picker opens, and I pick the matching snowboard. <break time="1.0s" /> The preview shows the three boards.
