2. Source of the products (inventory)

The app does not supply, sell, source or fulfil any products and has no inventory. It recommends products that already exist in the merchant's own store. The merchant links each bundle and add-on to one of those products with Shopify's product picker. The app's only product access is read_products; it cannot create or edit products.

What you saw, and what we changed. Your store had products with vendor Sample (Mesh Wi-Fi, HDMI 2.1 cable, Cinema Kit and others). They came from an optional "sample catalogue" CSV that earlier builds offered: a file generated from the chosen template, which the merchant imported with Shopify's own product import so they could try the questionnaire before linking real products. The import created them as active, published products, so they appeared for sale with no stock behind them. That was our mistake.

Fix (deployed FIX_DATE): the sample catalogue is removed from the app: no CSV download, no import instructions, no "link samples" action. Setup step 4 now only links products the store already sells, and the listing says the app never adds products to a store. Products left over from an earlier import carry the tag bundle-configurator-sample and can be deleted from Products by filtering on that tag.
