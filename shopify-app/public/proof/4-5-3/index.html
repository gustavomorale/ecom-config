2. Source of the products (inventory)

The app does not supply, sell, source or fulfil any products and has no inventory. It recommends products that already exist in the merchant's own store, which the merchant links with Shopify's product picker (read_products). It cannot create or edit products: it has no write access to products.

The products seen in the app are one of two things:

WhatWhere it comes from
The merchant's own productsChosen by the merchant in setup step 4 with the Shopify product picker.
Optional sample productsA CSV the app generates from the template the merchant picked: one row per bundle and add-on, with the template's placeholder names and prices, vendor Sample, tag bundle-configurator-sample, and plain text placeholder images (generated by placehold.co, no photographs). The merchant imports it with Shopify's own Products, Import, and can delete the products at any time by that tag. There is no supplier and no stock behind them; they exist only so a store without matching products can try the questionnaire. The screencast shows the file, the import and the result (chapter 5).
