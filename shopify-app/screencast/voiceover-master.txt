Any Shopify store can use this app to help shoppers build the right cart. A few simple questions bring together the products that solve one real need, so a bigger decision becomes simple.

These are this store's own products. CraftFrame Bundle Quiz sells nothing and has no inventory. It only recommends products like these.

I install the app. It asks only to view products and the theme. There is no separate account or login: everything runs inside the Shopify admin.

The welcome page explains the three steps to go live, what is included, and the plans. This store is on the Free plan.

I click Start setup. Step one: your products.

A product finder gives each shopper one product. What do you sell only suggests the wording of the questions. I choose Sports and outdoors.

This is where every product in the quiz comes from: Shopify's own product picker, listing the products this store already sells.

I pick five snowboards.

Their names, photos and prices come straight from the catalogue.

Save and continue. Step two: the questions. Each answer recommends one or more of my products: I just click them. The product recommended most often wins. Each answer can show an emoji or a product photo.

A second question: how long have you been riding? The preview shows exactly what shoppers will see.

Save and continue.

Step three: go live. In the theme editor, I add the CraftFrame Bundle Quiz block as a section and save. The app never edits the theme itself.

Home shows the quiz is live.

Now, as a shopper.

I pick park and tricks, then a few seasons, and see my pick: a real snowboard from this store, with its real price and why it was chosen.

Add to cart opens checkout with exactly that product.

That is the full setup. Support: contact at craftframe dot agency.

This is just one example. Stores with a wider range of products benefit the most, because the quiz brings the right products together in one cart, which is built to help raise the average order value.
